Who we are

Flannel and Parkers

We're a five-piece covers band that only plays one decade, and makes no apology for it.

From the first Nirvana riff to the last Oasis chorus, we play the songs that soundtracked the 90s: the grunge, the Britpop, the pop-punk and the guilty-pleasure singalongs. Harriet out front, Alex and Jonny on guitars, James on bass, Dave on drums, and three of them singing. It gets loud.

We play pubs, festivals, weddings, birthdays and anywhere else that wants a dance floor full by the second song. Everything is live. Nothing is on a backing track.

  • Five-piece, fully live line-up
  • Up to 2 x 60-minute sets, or one long one
  • Full PA and lighting available
  • Public liability insured, PAT tested
